ABOUT THE HOUSE

A small room
with a long memory.

Maple & Steam is a fictional coffee house built as a front-end project. The goal was to make a café site that feels warm without using a single stock photograph.

OUR APPROACH

Good coffee.
No theatre.

The menu is short on purpose. Four coffee origins, a handful of teas, a bakery case, and a brunch list that changes with the season. If something is not working, it leaves the board.

The room is designed around conversation: low light, soft edges, and enough space to stay after the cup is empty. The visual language uses warm paper tones, terracotta, sage, and simple line drawings.

WHAT WE CARE ABOUT

Three house rules.

These are the decisions that shape the menu, the room, and the way the team works.

Rule 01

Source clearly

Every coffee has a named origin and a roast date. If we cannot explain where it came from, it does not go on the bar.

Rule 02

Make it here

Syrups, sauces, and the bakery case are made in-house. The short menu keeps the standard consistent.

Rule 03

Leave room

No rush, no two-hour table limit, no music loud enough to end a conversation. The room is part of the service.
