THE IDEA

A café that feels
like a pause.

Maple & Steam is built around a simple idea: good coffee does not need to be complicated. We keep the menu small, the sourcing clear, and the room comfortable enough to stay for one more cup.

Everything is made in-house — the syrups, the bakes, the slow mornings. No photographs on this site; the visual identity is built from type, colour, and line art.
